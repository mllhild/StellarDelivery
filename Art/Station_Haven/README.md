# Station_Haven

A large, non-rotating, run-down station ("HAVEN") that serves as a smuggler hub. It uses the fleet's toon style with the grime, rust, patch, scorch and neon materials from the earlier stations. It takes ships up to cruiser size.

- About 430 × 475 × 360 m including the spine and drydock. 1 Blender unit = 1 m.
- About 57.6k triangles (outline shells included), 286 objects under `Haven_Root`.
- Source: `Assets/Models/Station_Haven/Station_Haven.blend`. Unity: `Station_Haven.fbx` (same export settings as the ships). The toon materials are set up by `ToonMaterialPostprocessor`.

All pads are worn Elite-style pads: tiles that don't match, chipped borders, faded numbers, scorch marks, mostly dead edge lights, leaning holo signs and beacons.

## Clutter
- A crooked shanty spine of 14 stacked habs rises about 155 m above the roof, with bolt-on pods and windows that are partly lit. A bent mast, antennas and a dish sit on top.
- Neon signs on the spine read "HAVEN", "BAR 24H" and "NO ID? NO PROBLEM".
- Containers and tanks are bolted to the hull.
- The solar wings are bent, with one panel missing.
- Cables sag across the hull and debris floats around the station.

The renders show a cruiser inside the hangar on C1, a cruiser in the drydock, a destroyer on D1 and frigates on pads 01, 03 and 04.
