# Station_Outpost

Small space station in the fleet's anime/Starsector style, built from the same toon materials. Its four landing pads and hangars each take a frigate (`Frigate_Explorer`, 59 m).

- 243 m across the hangar arms and habitat ring, and 222 m tall from the lower spire to the antenna tip. 1 Blender unit = 1 m.
- About 19.7k triangles (outline shells included), 250 objects under `Station_Root`.
- Source: `Assets/Models/Station_Outpost/Station_Outpost.blend`. Unity: `Station_Outpost.fbx` (same export settings as the ships). The toon materials are set up by `ToonMaterialPostprocessor`.

## Landing pads and hangars (x4, numbered 01–04)
Each arm is an Elite Dangerous–style elevator pad on top of an anime-style open hangar.
- **Pad (roof):** a 76 × 46 m dark deck with a yellow border, an elevator seam, a centre line and touchdown mark, and a large painted pad number. White chevrons point toward the hub, and the long edges carry alternating glowing and orange lights.
- **Hangar (below):** about 91 m deep, 52 m wide and 32 m tall. It's open at the outer end with an orange door frame, a hazard-striped lintel and a retracted blast door. Inside there are blue ceiling light strips, yellow and orange floor guide lines, and green approach lights along the floor.
- **Approach aids:** a holographic pad-number sign on a post, red beacons on the outer corners, and a control booth with windows overlooking each pad from the hub.

## Station
- An octagonal spindle hub with orange bands and a control tower on top with wraparound windows, an antenna mast and beacons.
- A communications dish on the upper shoulder.
- An anime-style habitat ring (radius 100 m, about 44 m above the pads) on four spokes, with a window strip and an orange inner band.
- Four radiator fins with orange tips and a navy spire below.
- Hangar arms braced to the lower hub with struts.

The renders show frigates parked on pad 01, inside hangar 03 and on approach.
