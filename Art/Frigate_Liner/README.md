# Frigate_Liner

Civilian counterpart to the explorer of the same class, built for personal transport and pleasure cruises in the fleet's anime/Starsector toon style. It has a white hull with a light blue belly and accents, magenta markings and glowing glass observation decks, and it puts looks ahead of function. It still carries small turrets in blue slot rings for light self-defence. The nose points to -Y in Blender. The source `.blend` and Unity `.fbx` are in `Assets/Models/Frigate_Liner/` (same export settings as the other ships; materials are set up by `ToonMaterialPostprocessor`). New materials: `Civ_White`, `Civ_Blue`, `Civ_Magenta`, `Civ_Glass` (lightly emissive) and `Civ_Water`.

- Private yacht *AURORA*: about 59 m long (matches Frigate_Explorer), about 11k triangles, root `Frigate_Liner_Root`.
- Observation decks: a ribbed glass sky lounge on the bow, a dorsal glass dome and a promenade window band along both sides.
- Leisure: an aft sun deck with a pool and magenta loungers.
- Decor: a magenta and blue swoosh livery, a V-tail with magenta tips and lights, and a decorative halo ring around the stern.
- Propulsion: two engine nacelles on swept pylons plus a central engine.
- Armament: two small turret slots, one dorsal and one ventral.
