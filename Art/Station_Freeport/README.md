# Station_Freeport

A medium, non-rotating, run-down freeport ("FREEPORT – NO QUESTIONS ASKED") built inside the gutted hull of an old freighter. It uses the fleet's toon style plus the grime, rust, patch, scorch and neon materials from `Station_Hideout`. It takes both frigates and destroyers.

- About 281 × 268 × 124 m. 1 Blender unit = 1 m.
- About 42.8k triangles (outline shells included), 290 objects under `Freeport_Root`.
- Source: `Assets/Models/Station_Freeport/Station_Freeport.blend`. Unity: `Station_Freeport.fbx` (same export settings as the ships). The toon materials are set up by `ToonMaterialPostprocessor`.

## Berths
| Berth | Type | Size | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Bow hangar | Destroyer hangar | about 105 × 61 × 39 m | The freighter's bow is cut open. Inside: patchwork lining, two gantry cranes, crates, half-dead lights and green guide lights. The orange mouth frame has one rusted post and a missing hazard stripe. |
| Pad D1 | Destroyer pad | 120 × 62 m | Cantilevered off the port side on struts. |
| Pad 01 + "REPAIRS" shed | Frigate pad and hangar | 76 × 50 m pad, 65 × 50 × 24 m shed | Starboard arm. The ship lands on the pad and taxis into the shed. |
| Pad 02 + "DOCK" shed | Frigate pad and hangar | same | Starboard arm, mounted higher than pad 01. |
| Pad 03 | Frigate overflow pad | 76 × 46 m | On top of the bow. |

All pads are worn takes on Elite Dangerous pads: tiles that don't match and a few missing, chipped borders, faded numbers, scorch marks, mostly dead edge lights, leaning holo number signs and some broken beacons.

## Clutter
- A patchwork deck over the stern carries a shanty town of stacked habitats with lit and neon windows.
- A crooked, scaffolded control tower sits among them, next to the big "FREEPORT" neon billboard.
- An exposed-rib section shows where hull plating was stripped.
- The engines are dead, with one still glowing, and refinery tanks hang underneath.
- Antennas lean at odd angles, the dish is broken and the solar wings are bent.
- Pipes, sagging cables, floodlights and floating debris fill in the rest.

The renders show a frigate on pad 01, one in the DOCK shed and one on pad 03, a destroyer on pad D1 and a destroyer inside the bow hangar.
