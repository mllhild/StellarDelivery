# Frigate_Warship

Military counterpart to the explorer of the same class, in the fleet's anime/Starsector toon style. Dark grey hull with dark blue and white markings. The faction emblem (see `Art/Station_Bastion/faction_emblem_reference.png`) is built as geometry, so it needs no textures. Nose points to -Y in Blender. Source `.blend` and Unity `.fbx` are in `Assets/Models/Frigate_Warship/` (same export settings as the other ships; materials set up by `ToonMaterialPostprocessor`). New materials: `War_Grey`, `War_Grey2`, `War_Dark` and `War_Blue`.

- Hull number FF-01, about 59 m long (matches Frigate_Explorer), about 7.8k triangles, root `Frigate_Warship_Root`.
- Armament: a forward twin turret, a ventral turret, two fixed prow guns, missile racks on the side armour modules and four point-defence mounts.
- Markings: a simplified emblem on its flag on the dorsal deck and on both sides of the tail fin, an emblem mark on each side module, blue bow chevrons, white pinstripes and a blue and white flank band.
- Two main engines and one auxiliary engine, a stepped bridge with a mast, and swept ventral fins.
