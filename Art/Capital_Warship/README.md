# Capital_Warship

Military counterpart to the explorer of the same class, in the fleet's anime/Starsector toon style. Dark grey hull with dark blue and white markings. The faction emblem (see `Art/Station_Bastion/faction_emblem_reference.png`) is built as geometry, so it needs no textures. Nose points to -Y in Blender. Source `.blend` and Unity `.fbx` are in `Assets/Models/Capital_Warship/` (same export settings as the other ships; materials set up by `ToonMaterialPostprocessor`). New materials: `War_Grey`, `War_Grey2`, `War_Dark` and `War_Blue`.

- Hull number BB-04, about 350 m long (matches Capital_Explorer) plus a spinal cannon reaching about 22 m past the bow. About 140 m wide, about 22.6k triangles, root `Capital_Warship_Root`.
- A flagship battleship-carrier: a central battle hull plus two outboard carrier hulls on pylons, each with a lit strike-craft hangar mouth at the front.
- Armament: a spinal super-cannon, four heavy twin turrets (two forward superfiring, two aft), two ventral turrets, four turrets on the outboard hulls, a 50-cell VLS field and ten point-defence mounts.
- A raised command tower with a bridge, rangefinders and a radar dish.
- Markings: the full emblem on its flag on the dorsal deck, both bow flanks and the outer flanks of the carrier hulls, the simplified emblem on both sides of the tail fin, an emblem mark on the tower, hull numbers and blue and white stripes.
- Seven engines: six in the main block and one on each outboard hull.

`render_fleet_lineup.png` shows the four warships (front) next to the four explorers (back).
