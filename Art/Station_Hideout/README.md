# Station_Hideout

A small, run-down smuggler outpost built into an asteroid. It does not rotate. It uses the same toon style as the fleet, with extra materials for rock, rust, grime, teal patches, scorch marks and neon. It has one landing pad and hangar, sized for `Frigate_Explorer` (59 m).

- About 250 × 131 × 144 m, including the cantilevered pad. 1 Blender unit = 1 m.
- About 26.8k triangles (outline shells included), 291 objects under `Hideout_Root`.
- Source: `Assets/Models/Station_Hideout/Station_Hideout.blend`. Unity: `Station_Hideout.fbx` (same export settings as the ships). The toon materials are set up by `ToonMaterialPostprocessor`. The new materials keep their colours and neon emission.

## Landing pad and hangar
- **Pad 01:** a 78 × 50 m cantilever deck on truss arms bolted into the rock. It is a worn take on an Elite Dangerous pad. The deck tiles don't match and one is missing. The yellow border is chipped, the "01" painted on the deck has faded, and there are scorch marks from landings. Many edge lights are dead, a few still glow yellow and one blinks red. A holo number sign leans on a bent post, one corner beacon is broken, and there is a refuelling boom with a rusty tank.
- **Hangar:** about 67 m deep, 55 m wide and 33 m tall, cut into the asteroid. A makeshift tunnel collar covers the rough rock edge, with an orange frame, a hazard lintel missing a stripe and a "DOCK 01" neon sign. Inside, the wall panels don't match, some ceiling lights have died, and the floor markings are worn. There are also crates, fuel drums and a gantry crane.

## Outpost clutter
A patchwork deck on girders carries a scaffolded control tower built from stacked blocks, with an "OPEN" neon sign. Around it sit container habitats stacked at odd angles with lit windows, and large habitat blocks are bolted to the rock on struts. There's also an observation module over the hangar mouth, tanks, pipes and sagging cables. The antennas lean at odd angles, the dish is tilted and broken, and one solar wing is bent. A cannibalised old ship hulk is grafted onto the back, and debris floats nearby.
