# Destroyer_Warship

Military counterpart to the explorer of the same class, in the fleet's anime/Starsector toon style. Dark grey hull with dark blue and white markings. The faction emblem (see `Art/Station_Bastion/faction_emblem_reference.png`) is built as geometry, so it needs no textures. Nose points to -Y in Blender. Source `.blend` and Unity `.fbx` are in `Assets/Models/Destroyer_Warship/` (same export settings as the other ships; materials set up by `ToonMaterialPostprocessor`). New materials: `War_Grey`, `War_Grey2`, `War_Dark` and `War_Blue`.

- Hull number DD-02, about 95 m long (matches Destroyer_Explorer), about 12.4k triangles, root `Destroyer_Warship_Root`.
- Armament: two forward twin turrets in a superfiring pair, an aft turret, a ventral turret, four secondary turrets on the side armour modules, four torpedo tubes in the prow, a 12-cell VLS block and six point-defence mounts.
- Markings: the simplified emblem on its flag on both side modules, the dorsal deck and the tail fin, plus blue chevrons and white pinstripes.
- Three engines, a bridge with a mast, and ventral and horizontal fins.
