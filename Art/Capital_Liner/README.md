# Capital_Liner

Civilian counterpart to the explorer of the same class, built for personal transport and pleasure cruises in the fleet's anime/Starsector toon style. It has a white hull with a light blue belly and accents, magenta markings and glowing glass observation decks, and it puts looks ahead of function. It still carries small turrets in blue slot rings for light self-defence. The nose points to -Y in Blender. The source `.blend` and Unity `.fbx` are in `Assets/Models/Capital_Liner/` (same export settings as the other ships; materials are set up by `ToonMaterialPostprocessor`). New materials: `Civ_White`, `Civ_Blue`, `Civ_Magenta`, `Civ_Glass` (lightly emissive) and `Civ_Water`.

- Grand liner *EMPRESS*: about 350 m long (matches Capital_Explorer), about 35k triangles, root `Capital_Liner_Root`.
- Layout: a trimaran with a central hull carrying a three-deck tiered superstructure, plus two outrigger hulls.
- Observation decks:
  - A big ribbed glass "crystal palace" atrium on the top deck.
  - A glass panorama lounge at the front of every deck.
  - Four glass skybridge galleries linking the outriggers to the main hull.
  - A grand observation ring: a non-rotating glass promenade torus, about 200 m across, around the whole ship.
- Leisure: three pools on the aft lido and one on the second deck, with loungers and parasols.
- Decor: 24 tender pods, a magenta halo ring, tall magenta-tipped fins on the outriggers and the main hull, and a swoosh livery.
- Propulsion: three main engines plus one in each outrigger.
- Armament: five small turret slots.
