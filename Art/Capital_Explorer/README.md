# Capital_Explorer

Capital-class exploration and cargo mothership in the same anime/Starsector style as the frigate, destroyer and cruiser, and built from the same materials.

- 349 m long including the bow sensor spear (hull about 305 m), 135 m wide across the wings and 59 m tall. That is 1.9× the cruiser's 181.7 m. 1 Blender unit = 1 m.
- About 65k triangles (outline shells included), 353 objects under `Capital_Root`. The containers are merged into four meshes.
- Source: `Assets/Models/Capital_Explorer/Capital_Explorer.blend`. Unity: `Capital_Explorer.fbx` (same export settings as the others). The toon materials are set up by `ToonMaterialPostprocessor`.

## Layout
- **Catamaran mothership:** a central command hull flanked by two cargo hulls. Wing-bridges join them, and forward deltas give an arrowhead silhouette.
- **Cargo:** 24 open-frame bays on top of the cargo hulls hold 322 containers. The cargo hulls have six side loading doors each, and the central keel has nine ventral bay doors.
- **Docking:** a large lit docking bay on each cargo hull's outer flank, big enough for the frigate or shuttles.
- **Exploration:** a 12 m-radius survey dish, a spoked sensor ring on an aft mast, a long bow sensor spear with collars, sensor blisters and a ventral sensor dome.
- **Command:** a three-tier tower with bridge wings and a tall antenna mast. Two landing pads on the flight deck.
- **Propulsion and defence:** 8 central engines plus 4 on each cargo hull, large canted fins and wings, and 12 point-defence turrets.
