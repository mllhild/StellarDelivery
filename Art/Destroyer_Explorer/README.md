# Destroyer_Explorer

Destroyer-class exploration and cargo ship in the same anime/Starsector style as `Frigate_Explorer`, and built from the same materials.

- 94.5 m long, 45.4 m wide across the wings and 20.8 m tall. That is 1.6× the frigate's 59.2 m. 1 Blender unit = 1 m.
- About 14.8k triangles (outline shells included), 277 objects under `Destroyer_Root`.
- Source: `Assets/Models/Destroyer_Explorer/Destroyer_Explorer.blend`. Unity: `Destroyer_Explorer.fbx` (same export settings as the frigate). The toon materials are set up automatically by `ToonMaterialPostprocessor`.

## Layout
- Split twin-prong bow with a three-lens sensor array between the prongs and a sensor dome underneath.
- Large exploration dish ahead of a three-tier bridge with two masts and a small secondary dish.
- Dorsal survey-drone hangar with a landing pad on its roof.
- Two side cargo modules with open container racks (42 containers), a ventral cargo hold with bay doors, and engines on the modules.
- Five main engines, swept wings, canted dorsal and ventral fins, and four point-defence turrets.
