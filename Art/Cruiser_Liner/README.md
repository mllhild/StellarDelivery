# Cruiser_Liner

Civilian counterpart to the explorer of the same class, built for personal transport and pleasure cruises in the fleet's anime/Starsector toon style. It has a white hull with a light blue belly and accents, magenta markings and glowing glass observation decks, and it puts looks ahead of function. It still carries small turrets in blue slot rings for light self-defence. The nose points to -Y in Blender. The source `.blend` and Unity `.fbx` are in `Assets/Models/Cruiser_Liner/` (same export settings as the other ships; materials are set up by `ToonMaterialPostprocessor`). New materials: `Civ_White`, `Civ_Blue`, `Civ_Magenta`, `Civ_Glass` (lightly emissive) and `Civ_Water`.

- Cruise ship *CELESTE*: about 182 m long (matches Cruiser_Explorer), about 22k triangles, root `Cruiser_Liner_Root`.
- Observation decks:
  - A tiered superstructure of three stacked decks, each with window bands and a glass panorama lounge at its front.
  - A ribbed glass atrium dome on the top deck.
  - A bridge window band.
- Leisure: two pools on the aft lido and one on the second deck roof, with loungers and parasols.
- Decor: 20 tender pods along the sides, double halo rings, a magenta-tipped V-tail and a swoosh livery.
- Propulsion: two large nacelles plus three main engines.
- Armament: five small turret slots.
