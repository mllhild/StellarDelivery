# Cruiser_Warship

Military counterpart to the explorer of the same class, in the fleet's anime/Starsector toon style. Dark grey hull with dark blue and white markings. The faction emblem (see `Art/Station_Bastion/faction_emblem_reference.png`) is built as geometry, so it needs no textures. Nose points to -Y in Blender. Source `.blend` and Unity `.fbx` are in `Assets/Models/Cruiser_Warship/` (same export settings as the other ships; materials set up by `ToonMaterialPostprocessor`). New materials: `War_Grey`, `War_Grey2`, `War_Dark` and `War_Blue`.

- Hull number CA-03, about 182 m long (matches Cruiser_Explorer) plus a spinal cannon reaching about 14 m past the bow. About 14.8k triangles, root `Cruiser_Warship_Root`.
- Armament: an anime-style spinal cannon in the prow, three heavy twin turrets (two forward superfiring, one aft), two ventral turrets, four secondary turrets, a 24-cell VLS block and eight point-defence mounts.
- Strike-craft hangars: four bays in each side armour module, with red door lights.
- Markings: the full emblem on its flag on both bow flanks and the dorsal deck, and the simplified emblem on the tail fin.
- Four engines (2×2), a bridge with rangefinder arms, and swept fins.
