# Destroyer_Liner

Civilian counterpart to the explorer of the same class, built for personal transport and pleasure cruises in the fleet's anime/Starsector toon style. It has a white hull with a light blue belly and accents, magenta markings and glowing glass observation decks, and it puts looks ahead of function. It still carries small turrets in blue slot rings for light self-defence. The nose points to -Y in Blender. The source `.blend` and Unity `.fbx` are in `Assets/Models/Destroyer_Liner/` (same export settings as the other ships; materials are set up by `ToonMaterialPostprocessor`). New materials: `Civ_White`, `Civ_Blue`, `Civ_Magenta`, `Civ_Glass` (lightly emissive) and `Civ_Water`.

- Fast liner *SERENADE*: about 95 m long (matches Destroyer_Explorer), about 14.8k triangles, root `Destroyer_Liner_Root`.
- Observation decks:
  - A long upper-deck superstructure with two rows of windows.
  - A big ribbed glass lounge at the front of the upper deck.
  - Twin glass domes on its roof.
  - Two rows of promenade windows on the hull.
- Leisure: an aft lido with a pool, loungers and parasols.
- Decor: double halo rings (blue and magenta), a swept dorsal fin and side fins with magenta tips, and a swoosh livery.
- Propulsion: two nacelles plus a central engine.
- Armament: three small turret slots.
